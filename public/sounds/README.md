# Typing sound samples

`typewriter1.wav` through `typewriter4.wav` are CC0 recordings by Cassie-OrbitGames, downloaded from [OpenGameArt — Typewriter sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/typewriter-sounds).

They are short, locally bundled key samples. The editor varies the sample, speed, volume, and tone filter for its different typing styles.

`pencil-write.ogg` is a CC0 pencil-writing recording curated by AntumDeluge in [OpenGameArt — Pencil Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/pencil-sounds). `retro-key.wav` is a CC0 retro selection sound by cosmac from [OpenGameArt — Selection Beep](https://opengameart.org/content/selection-beep).
